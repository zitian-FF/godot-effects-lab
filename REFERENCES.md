# Reference notes

Official game footage is reference material only, not a distributed texture source. The runtime contains original authored silhouettes, shaders and particle arrangements.

- [Hu Tao: Let the Living Beware](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qrH9vMZBwAk) — fire / butterfly motif target.
- [Raiden Shogun: Judgment of Euthymia](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mvrW4aKwAXw&t=104s) — **combat frame at1:44 visually inspected**: narrow white/cyan stroke, wider saturated violet response, separate pink-violet fragments. Used to revise the initially underlit cut.
- [Ayaka: Camellia in Winter Snow](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5RCsr1Y8UZA) — ice petal / frost motif target.
- [Kokomi: A Thousand Waves Under the Moon](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fVf0RB3nvOU) — water restoration / jellyfish motif target.
- [Jiyan: Through the Darkest of Nights](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wnxtQsHOy1k) — wind sword and thrust motif target; dragon / character animation is outside the isolated reconstruction.
- [Chixia: Gallant Blaze](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j6HReyWAM-s) — stylized firearm motif target; the lab implements an original gas / pressure / smoke sequence.
- [Changli: Seize the Initiative](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jYjxjy1l6Co&t=140s) — **combat at2:00 and release at2:20 visually inspected**. Combat uses thin near-white leading edges and translucent saturated red wakes; later release uses a hot gold wing-like fire silhouette with a large pressure arc. The lab reconstructs wing opening, fevered feather breakup and pressure expansion, with denser original texture detail.

The linked targets are not a claim that every frame of every demo was watched or measured. Sequence timing is authored for this lab; it is not measured from the videos. Local reference screenshots are kept in the work output for critique and are not bundled with the game.
